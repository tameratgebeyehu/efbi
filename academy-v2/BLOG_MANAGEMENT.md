# EFBI article publishing guide

The Blog workspace is available only inside the localhost Admin Studio. It is not part of the public learner website.

## Publish an article

1. Open **Blog** in Admin Studio and choose **New article**.
2. Enter a clear title, short summary, category, author, reading time, color, and article body.
3. Use the preview beside the editor to check the public appearance.
4. Save the draft. Unsaved writing is also kept in this browser for recovery after an interruption.
5. Mark the article **Ready for review** only after checking every link and statement.
6. Publish it. The article then appears on the public Blog page and receives release 1.

Only published snapshots are public. Drafts, recovery text, and release history remain private to the administrator.

## Correct a published article

Open the published article, make the correction, save it as a draft, review it again, and publish it. This creates the next numbered release and updates the public copy. It does not change the earlier release.

## Remove an article from public view

Open the published article, make sure there are no unsaved changes, type the exact confirmation phrase shown by Admin Studio, and choose **Unpublish article**. The public copy disappears, but the private draft, releases, and audit record remain. The article can later be corrected and republished as a new release.

## Article formatting

Plain text is enough. Separate paragraphs with a blank line. The editor also accepts:

- `## Heading` for a main section
- `### Heading` for a smaller section
- lines beginning with `- ` for a bullet list

Raw HTML is not supported. This keeps public articles predictable and safe.

## Featured articles

The newest published article marked **Featured** appears at the top of the Blog page. If several articles are marked Featured, the newest one wins.

## Before publishing

- Confirm names, dates, facts, and external links.
- Do not include student email addresses, phone numbers, private stories, or other personal data.
- Use only material EFBI owns or has permission to publish.
- Preview on both a phone-sized and desktop-sized screen.
- Correct errors through a new release; never try to edit release history.
