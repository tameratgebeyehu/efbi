## Notice requirements to verify

The learner notice should plainly identify EFBI, purposes, categories, lawful bases, recipients, international transfers, retention periods, required versus optional fields, rights, complaint route, certificate-publication choice, and the consequences of deletion. Do not open enrollment with placeholder contacts or undefined retention language.

The 14-day request target shown in Admin Studio is an internal EFBI service target. The Proclamation uses the standard “without excessive delay” for erasure; the interface must not describe 14 days as a statutory deadline.
