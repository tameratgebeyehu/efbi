# EFBI pre-production privacy and safeguarding gate

Status: **blocked — external approval required**

This checklist supports a professional review; it is not legal advice and does not replace an Ethiopian lawyer, the competent data-protection authority, or an EFBI safeguarding professional.

## Decisions that must be signed off

- [ ] Identify the EFBI legal entity or responsible individual acting as data controller.
- [ ] Publish a working privacy contact and escalation contact.
- [ ] Confirm the lawful basis for account, progress, project, review, certificate, security, and audit processing.
- [ ] Decide whether learners under 18 may enroll. If they may, approve age-appropriate notices, consent/guardian rules, safeguarding escalation, and minimum-data design before collecting age information.
- [ ] Approve the `efbi-retention-v1` periods or replace them with reviewed periods.
- [ ] Approve the narrow certificate-verification retention purpose and duration, including revoked and replaced credentials.
- [ ] Approve when legal, safety, fraud, or integrity holds may be used and who reviews them every 30 days.
- [ ] Review Google/Firebase, YouTube, and email delivery as processors or recipients, including international data-transfer requirements.
- [ ] Approve access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, refusal, and complaint procedures.
- [ ] Approve a breach-response plan, including the Proclamation's notification requirements and an always-available incident owner.
- [ ] Complete a data-protection impact/risk assessment for learners, minors, public certificate names, reviewer notes, and external evidence links.

## Notice requirements to verify

The learner notice should plainly identify EFBI, purposes, categories, lawful bases, recipients, international transfers, retention periods, required versus optional fields, rights, complaint route, certificate-publication choice, and the consequences of deletion. Do not open enrollment with placeholder contacts or undefined retention language.

The 14-day request target shown in Admin Studio is an internal EFBI service target. The Proclamation uses the standard “without excessive delay” for erasure; the interface must not describe 14 days as a statutory deadline.

- screenshots of learner request states and the private operator queue
- the final learner privacy notice and safeguarding procedure
