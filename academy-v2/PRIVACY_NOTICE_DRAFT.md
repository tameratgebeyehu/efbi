# EFBI learner privacy notice — review draft

Status: **not approved and not for publication**

This plain-language draft records what the current EFBI Academy development system does. Ethiopian privacy/legal and safeguarding reviewers must approve it before public enrollment.

## Who is responsible

EFBI Academy is led by **Tamerat Gebeyehu, CEO and Founder**. Questions about personal information or deletion requests can be sent to **efbi.academy@gmail.com**.

The review must confirm the final legal name and capacity of the person or organization acting as data controller.

## Information EFBI uses

The planned service uses:

- your account name and email address;
- course progress and completed lessons;
- project answers and evidence links you choose to submit;
- private and learner-visible review records;
- your separate choice of public certificate name, if you request a certificate;
- security, role, consent, certificate, and deletion audit records needed to protect the service.

EFBI does not need a phone number, exact home address, government ID, full birth date, health information, or payment information for the current pilot. Learners should not place sensitive personal information in projects or evidence links.

## Why EFBI uses it

EFBI uses the minimum information needed to create and secure an account, save learning progress, review submitted work, issue a requested certificate, verify a certificate, answer support and privacy requests, and investigate genuine safety or integrity concerns.

The final notice must list the reviewed lawful basis for each purpose before this section is published.

## Learners under 16

EFBI intends to support learners under 16 and learners aged 16 or older. Ordinary self-registration will not be available to someone under 16. A parent, guardian, or tutor must complete the reviewed authorization process before EFBI creates the learner account.

EFBI will not use a minor's information for marketing, profiling, or combining advertising profiles. The final process must explain how EFBI verifies age and guardian authorization, how authorization can be withdrawn, and how a child or guardian can ask for help.

## Certificates

A certificate is not published automatically. After approved work, the learner separately chooses the name that may appear in public verification. Public verification is limited to that name, course, issue date, credential ID, replacement link, and current status. Projects, scores, email addresses, user IDs, and reviewer details stay private.

An under-16 learner's certificate choice requires the separately reviewed guardian path. The final notice must explain the approved retention period for certificate evidence, including revoked and replaced credentials.

## Service providers and transfers

The current design uses Google Firebase for sign-in and data storage, YouTube for lesson video delivery, and email providers for account or support messages. These services may process information outside Ethiopia. EFBI must complete and publish the reviewed processor, recipient, location, safeguard, and transfer information before enrollment.

## How long information is kept

The current development schedule is in `RETENTION_AND_DELETION.md`. It is provisional. The final notice must state the externally reviewed periods in plain language.

## Your choices and rights

Learners can request access, correction, restriction, or deletion by contacting **efbi.academy@gmail.com**. The final notice must explain every applicable right, identity-check process, response process, complaint route, consent withdrawal, and any lawful exception.

## Development boundary

The learner platform remains in development. This draft does not authorize public enrollment, under-16 account creation, production data collection, or publication of a learner's certificate name.
